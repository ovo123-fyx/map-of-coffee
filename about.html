About Us

We're a small team of coffee lovers mapping every great café in Brisbane — one cup at a time.

Our Story

Map of Coffee started in 2025 as a side project between three university friends who shared one habit — drinking too much coffee while studying. We grew tired of wandering between cafés hoping the next one would be "the one", so we started writing down what we liked about each place: the beans, the music, the seats by the window. That notebook turned into this website.

Today, we curate 120+ specialty coffee shops across Brisbane. Every café on this site has been visited by at least one of us. We don't accept payment for listings — only honest cups and honest words.
