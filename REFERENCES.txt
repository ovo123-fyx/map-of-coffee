DESIGN PRINCIPLES
----------------------------------------------------------

Nielsen, J. (1994). 10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design.
  Nielsen Norman Group.
  https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/

  Applied throughout the website design, particularly in:
  - Navigation consistency across all 5 pages
  - User control (search reset, back buttons)
  - Recognition rather than recall (visible labels on all buttons)
  - Feedback (hover states, focus rings, "no results" message)
